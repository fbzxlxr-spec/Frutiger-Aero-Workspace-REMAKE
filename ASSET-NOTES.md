The four recovered raster wallpapers are copied byte-for-byte. The desktop and screensaver display the full image stretched to the viewport so the source image is not cropped or edited.

## Icon artwork

The source JPEG icon art and uploaded icon sheet are retained in `assets/originals/`. Desktop PNGs in `assets/icons/` are transparent derivatives made by removing only background pixels connected to the image edges. The sunflower, settings gear, people, controller, fish, messages, Discord, music, and Finder artwork is preserved.

## Audio

- `assets/audio/startup-original.mp3` is the recovered original workspace startup sound.
- `aero-track-01.wav` through `aero-track-10.wav` are newly generated original synth tracks for the Aero Playlist and Aero Dash levels.
- `victory-cheer.wav` is a newly generated original win jingle.
